# Final Year Design Project Report Instructions

Use `FYDP_Report_Template.docx` as the official formatting and structural reference.

## Rules

1. Follow the exact section order from the template.
2. Preserve heading hierarchy.
3. Follow the formatting style of the template.
4. Do not remove required sections.
5. Generate content academically.
6. Maintain consistency throughout the report.
7. Use proper figure and table captions.
8. Keep placeholders for information that has not been provided.
9. Do not invent project data, research results, or references.
10. Generate the report section by section.

## Required Workflow

Before writing:
- Analyze the complete template structure.
- Identify all chapters and subsections.
- Identify formatting patterns.
- Identify required front-matter sections.

While writing:
- Follow the exact template structure.
- Generate content appropriate to each section.
- Maintain academic writing style.

Before finalizing:
- Check section completeness.
- Check heading hierarchy.
- Check formatting consistency.
- Check references and citations.
