## **🧾 1\. Project Title**

**Early Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease Using Peptide Sequences with Machine Learning and Deep Learning**

## **📌 2\. Background & Problem Statement**

Alzheimer’s Disease is a **progressive brain disorder** that is usually detected late using MRI or cognitive tests.

👉 Problem:

* Current diagnosis \= expensive & late  
* Need \= **early, low-cost detection**

👉 Solution:  
Use **peptide sequences (amyloid-beta, tau)** \+ AI to detect disease early
